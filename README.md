# Silmer · Silk Meridian

**Iran trade data hub, in English and Persian.** Live site: https://amttib.github.io/Simer/

Silmer is an interactive statistics book of Iran's foreign trade. It covers exports and imports by product (8-digit tariff line), country, customs office, province and month. It also shows world commodity prices, Iran Mercantile Exchange prices, official exchange rates, export opportunities and the world market for every product.

This repository holds only the built site (HTML, JSON data and photos). It runs on GitHub Pages without a server. `live.json` carries the Iran Mercantile Exchange's latest trades and the Central Bank's rates, and is refreshed every 15 minutes.

## سیلمر · مرکز داده‌های تجارت ایران

آمارنامه‌ی تعاملی تجارت خارجی ایران: صادرات و واردات به تفکیک کالا (کد تعرفه‌ی ۸ رقمی)، کشور، گمرک، استان و ماه؛ قیمت جهانی کالاهای پایه، بورس کالای ایران و نرخ رسمی ارز؛ فرصت‌های صادراتی و بازار جهانی هر کالا. سایت به انگلیسی و فارسی است.

## Data sources · منابع داده

- Islamic Republic of Iran Customs Administration: yearly and year-to-date foreign trade statistics (irica.gov.ir)
- Trade Promotion Organization of Iran: exports by province (tpo.ir)
- Iran Mercantile Exchange: monthly physical-market reports (ime.co.ir)
- Central Bank of the Islamic Republic of Iran: official exchange rates (cbi.ir)
- The World Bank, Commodity Price Data (The Pink Sheet), CC BY 4.0
- The Growth Lab at Harvard University, The Atlas of Economic Complexity
- UN Comtrade
